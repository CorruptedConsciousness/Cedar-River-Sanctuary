Cedar River Sanctuary

A privately operated wildlife observation and environmental monitoring station near Michigan's Cedar River.

Live cameras and field data coming soon

Observing the Cedar River corridor

Cedar River Sanctuary is a year-round wildlife monitoring project documenting the animals, weather, and natural soundscape surrounding a forested property near the Cedar River in Buckeye Township, Michigan.

The project

Live wildlife cameras

Multiple camera views will document birds, squirrels, mammals, and seasonal wildlife activity.

Environmental monitoring

Weather conditions and environmental observations will provide context for activity at the site.

Open data

Selected observations, recordings, and datasets will be published for public, educational, and research use.
